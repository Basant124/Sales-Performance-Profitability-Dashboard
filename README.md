# Sales Dashboard

## Overview

The **Sales Dashboard** provides an interactive overview of sales performance across different time periods, regions, products, categories, and states.

It is designed to help users quickly identify sales trends, high-performing products, and regional performance.

## Key Metrics

The dashboard displays the following high-level KPIs:

- **Discount:** 1,561
- **Profit:** 286,397
- **Profit Margin:** 12.47%
- **Quantity Sold:** 37,873

## Dashboard Visualizations

### 1. Total Sales By Year

A monthly sales trend showing how total sales change over time across multiple years.

**Purpose:**
- Identify sales trends
- Detect peaks and drops
- Compare performance across years

### 2. Total Sales By Quarter

A pie chart showing the distribution of sales across four quarters:

| Quarter | Sales |
|---------|------:|
| Q1 | 48,024 |
| Q2 | 55,285 |
| Q3 | 72,467 |
| Q4 | 110,622 |

**Key Insight:** Q4 generates the highest sales, while Q1 has the lowest.

### 3. Total Sales By Region

Regional sales performance is presented using a pie chart.

| Region | Sales |
|--------|------:|
| West | $725.46K |
| East | $678.78K |
| Central | $501.24K |

**Key Insight:** The West region has the highest sales, while the South has the lowest.

### 4. Top 10 Products by Sales

A bar chart displays the ten products with the highest sales.

The leading product generates approximately **$61.60K** in sales, followed by products generating approximately **$27.45K, $22.64K, $21.87K**, and others.

**Purpose:**
- Identify best-selling products
- Support inventory planning
- Understand product-level performance

  - Chairs

### 6. Total Sales By State

A geographic map displays sales distribution across U.S. states.

**Purpose:**
- Identify high-performing states
- Analyze geographic sales patterns
- Support regional sales strategies

## Key Insights

Based on the dashboard:

1. **Q4 is the strongest quarter**, accounting for the largest share of quarterly sales.
2. **The West region leads regional sales** at approximately $725.46K.
3. **The South region has the lowest regional sales** at approximately $391.72K.
4. **Technology products are a major contributor** to sales.
5. The dashboard's **12.47% profit margin** provides an overview of overall profitability.
6. Sales vary considerably from month to month, indicating seasonal and/or product-driven fluctuations.
